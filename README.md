# Indian-Market-Capitalization-Analysis
Comparative Analysis of Financial Performance Across Market Capitalization Indices in India

Tools Used:
- Excel - Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Power BI - Graphical representation and visualization of trends, patterns, and relationships among the indices.

Dataset:
- NSE (2015–2025), 3 Indices used are:
- NIFTY 50
- NIFTY Midcap 150
- NIFTY Smallcap 100

Analysis:
- Descriptive Statistics - Average returns, volatility, and distribution of returns.
- Correlation Analysis - Examine the relationship and co-movement.
- Regression Analysis - Assess the dependency of mid-cap and small-cap indices on large-cap index movements.

Key Findings:
Return Performance
- NIFTY Midcap 150 delivered the highest average returns among the three market-cap segments, indicating stronger growth potential.
  
Risk Analysis
- NIFTY Smallcap 100 exhibited the highest volatility, making it the riskiest segment despite its growth opportunities.
- NIFTY 50 is characterized by stability, lower volatility, and consistent performance, making it suitable for conservative investors.
  
Correlation Insights
- Mid-cap and small-cap indices showed a strong positive relationship, suggesting similar responses to market conditions.
- Large-cap indices maintained only moderate correlation with mid- and small-cap segments, creating diversification opportunities for investors.
  
Regression Analysis
- Large-cap market movements significantly influenced both mid-cap and small-cap returns.
- However, regression results indicated that a substantial portion of return variation was driven by factors beyond large-cap performance, highlighting independent market dynamics.

Investment Insight
- The analysis suggests that the mid-cap segment offered the most balanced risk-return profile, combining higher returns with manageable risk.
- A portfolio that includes a mix of large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap investments can help optimize returns while effectively managing risk.
